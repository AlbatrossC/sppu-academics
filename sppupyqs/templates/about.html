About SPPU PYQs

Previous year question papers, organized for actual exam preparation.

SPPU PYQs is a free website for Savitribai Phule Pune University students. It brings previous year question papers into a cleaner interface where students can search, view, compare, and download papers without first digging through scattered folders or downloading every PDF just to check its contents.

Why this site exists

Previous year papers are extremely useful during exam preparation, but the way they are usually shared is not always convenient. A student may have to browse a large Drive folder, open multiple college pages, or download several files before finding the correct subject, year, or exam type.

This site improves that experience by presenting the available papers in a structured and accessible format. It keeps the focus on the student workflow: choose the pattern, branch, semester, and subject; open the paper directly in the browser; compare papers when needed; and download only the files that are useful.

What the site improves

The site is built around practical exam-preparation needs instead of acting as a plain list of PDF links.

Browser PDF viewing

Open papers directly in the browser and check them before downloading, which is especially useful on mobile devices and low-storage systems.

Clearer paper details

Subject, exam type, and year information are presented through the interface so students can identify the right paper faster.

Side-by-side comparison

The split layout helps students compare papers or questions from different years without switching between tabs repeatedly.

Selective downloads

Students can download papers based on available categories such as insem, endsem, or other exam types.

Paper sources

The PDFs served through SPPU PYQs are collected from publicly available academic resources. A primary reference is the ZEAL COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING & RESEARCH, PUNE downloads library, which links to a public Google Drive folder containing SPPU question papers.

I do not own the question papers. If a paper is missing from the main collection, I may add it from another publicly available external source so students can find a more complete set from one place.

Free and open to use

SPPU PYQs is completely free to use. The website source is open, and you are free to host it as it is if you want to run your own copy. You may also download papers from this website and publish them on your own site, although I recommend using the original Drive folder or original source wherever possible when building your own collection.
